Before you contact us

A few practical
questions.

I’m not sure which practice area I need.

Call 01 621 9450 or email info@obriensolicitors.com with a brief outline. You can ask the practice whether it can help with your enquiry.

What should I include in my first enquiry?

Your contact details, the general nature of the matter and any important dates. Keep your initial message brief, and ask the practice how to share documents before sending sensitive information.

How can I find out about fees?

Ask the practice about the likely scope of work and fees for your circumstances before giving instructions. This concept website does not publish prices or offer a quotation.
