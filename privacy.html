About this preview

Privacy &
concept notice.

This is an independent website redesign concept. It is not operated or approved by Niall O’Brien & Co. Solicitors.

Business information

Practice areas, address, telephone, fax, DX and email details have been taken from the business’s existing website, reviewed on 9 October 2026. Contact the practice to confirm current services and arrangements.

No legal advice

The service descriptions are introductory information. They do not constitute legal advice, assess a claim or create a solicitor–client relationship.

The email draft form

The form runs in your browser. It does not send entries to a form service, store them in a database or save them in browser storage. Submitting the form prepares a mailto link. Selecting that link opens your configured email app; you must review and send the message yourself.

After you open or send a draft, your email provider handles the message under its own policies. This preview has no access to that process. Please avoid including sensitive information in your initial enquiry.

Cookies, analytics and hosting

This preview includes no analytics, advertising scripts or embedded maps, and does not intentionally set cookies or use browser storage. A hosting provider may process technical request information to deliver pages. If hosted on GitHub Pages, consult GitHub’s privacy statement.

External links

Telephone and email links open applications on your device. The directions link opens Google Maps. These services are operated separately from this preview.

Images & identity

The Four Courts photograph, illustrative paperwork photograph and small brand mark are adapted from assets on the existing website. The Four Courts photograph depicts a public landmark; it does not depict the practice’s office. These assets are used here for a redesign demonstration. Their reuse rights must be confirmed before a business launch.

Before a business launch

The practice must approve the content, contact destinations and asset rights, and provide its current privacy notice, regulatory disclosures and any required legal wording. This concept notice is not a substitute for the practice’s own policies.
