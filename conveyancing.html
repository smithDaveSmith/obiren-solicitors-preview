01 / Practice area

Conveyancing & tax

Buying or selling property brings important legal decisions. Niall O’Brien & Co. can assist with the stages of a purchase or sale, mortgages and other property matters.

Buying, selling or refinancing?

How the practice can help.

  1. 01

    Purchasing a property

    Assistance with deposits, structural engineer instructions, local authority enquiries, investigation of title and contracts.

  2. 02

    Contracts & completion

    Special conditions, searches, lending requirements, completion of the transaction and registration of the dealing.

  3. 03

    Selling a property

    Obtaining title documents, preparing contracts, redeeming a mortgage and completing the sale.

  4. 04

    Mortgages & other property matters

    Mortgages, remortgages, top-up loans, lender security requirements, boundary disputes and the purchase of ground rents.

The practice also lists tax among its areas of expertise. Contact the office to discuss the scope of your tax enquiry.
